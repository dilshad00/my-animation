## Description
What if every rain cloud on Earth started pouring real blood, and the forecast said it wouldn't stop for 7 days? 🩸🌧️ We follow a week of blood rain day by day, using real science and real disasters to work out what would happen to our cities, rivers, farms and oceans.

"Blood rain" is a real phenomenon. Homer wrote about it, Richard the Lionheart's army was drenched by it in 1198, and in 2001 red rain fell over Kerala, India, for about two months. Scientists traced it to algae spores (Trentepohlia), and in Europe it's usually Saharan dust. But what if it were actual blood? From the metallic smell and predators going wild, to clogged drains, fly swarms, river fish kills, burned crops and ocean dead zones the size of New Jersey, this is what 7 days of blood rain would do to the planet.
