## 1. Visual direction

| | |
|---|---|
| **Style** | Premium flat-vector documentary: layered parallax backgrounds, soft shadows, rim light, colour grading per chapter |
| **Blood treatment** | Stylised, never gory: deep crimson liquid, glossy highlights, no injuries, no dead people. Dead fish are shown as simple belly-up silhouettes. This keeps the video advertiser-friendly on YouTube. |
| **Colour arc** | History chapters are warm sepia. Rules use a blueprint blue. Days 1–3 are a crimson storm grade. Days 4–6 are a murky green/brown "sick water" grade. Day 7 is a rust grade with sun breaking through. The conclusion is clean, bright blue. |
| **Recurring device** | A **calendar page tears off** at the start of every day ("DAY 1" … "DAY 7"), with a thunder hit. A small **"HYPOTHETICAL" / "REAL EVENT" tag** in the corner of every scene from Chapter 2 onward, so viewers always know which is which. |
| **Motion rule** | Every scene has at least: a camera move (push, pan, drift or follow), one animated element entering on a narration cue, and ambient motion (rain, particles, characters). |
