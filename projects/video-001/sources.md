## Standard textbook facts (no single citation needed)
- Rain forms from condensed water vapour; cells, proteins and salts do not evaporate.
- Shed blood normally starts to clot within minutes. Blood is several times more viscous than water.
- Blood agar is a standard lab medium for growing bacteria; fast bacteria (e.g. *E. coli*) can double about every 20 minutes in ideal conditions.
- Typical household sewage BOD is a few hundred mg/L (e.g. Metcalf & Eddy, *Wastewater Engineering*).
- Decomposing organic matter consumes dissolved oxygen (the basis of fish kills and dead zones).

## Our own calculations
- 7 days of global precipitation: 520,000 km³ × 7/365 ≈ **10,000 km³**. Over land: 116,500 × 7/365 ≈ **2,200 km³**.
- All human blood: ~8.2 billion people × ≤5 L ≈ 41 billion L ≈ **0.041 km³** (less than 1/20 km³). Children have less, so this is an upper bound.
- Ratio: 10,000 / 0.041 ≈ **240,000**, so "more than two hundred thousand times".
- 20-minute doubling: 2³⁰ ≈ 1 billion after 10 hours, so "billions in less than half a day, in theory".

## Hypothetical: clearly framed as such in the narration
Everything from "Chapter 2: The Rules" to "Day 7" is a thought experiment. The narration signals this with "hypothetical", "would", "could", "likely" and "no one can test…". The hypothetical claims are:
- how predators react worldwide, drain clogging and flooding, fly and scavenger booms
- failures at water treatment plants, crop overdose, problems with livestock
- dead zones forming along coastlines everywhere, and how long recovery takes.

Each is extrapolated from the real mechanisms above. None is presented as established science.
