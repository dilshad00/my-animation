## Description
What if humanity had never moved past simple stone tools? No metal, no wheels, no writing, no engines, no medicine, and no phone in your pocket.

Using real archaeology and history, this animated what-if shows how people would eat, travel, heal, fight and remember in a world where the clock stopped, and what Earth would look like tonight if it had. The surprising part: they would be just as intelligent as we are.

If everything vanished and you could save just one invention, which one would it be? Tell us in the comments.

The historical facts in this video come from the sources below. The alternate world is speculation, and the narration says so whenever it begins.
