### Description draft
No phones, no engines, no medicine, no writing, not even the wheel. What if humanity had never moved past simple stone tools? Using real archaeology and history, this animated what-if explores how we would eat, travel, heal, fight and remember in a world where the clock stopped, and what Earth might look like tonight.

The historical facts in this video come from the sources below. The alternate world is speculation, and the narration says so whenever it begins.
