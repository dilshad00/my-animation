### Description draft
The dead don't have children. Until one day, one of them did.

In a world overrun by the infected, a medic named Mara meets a zombie who doesn't attack. Months later, a scan shows two heartbeats. When her daughter is born, every zombie in the marsh falls silent and turns toward the settlement. Is the child human, zombie, or something new? And why are the zombies afraid of her?

This is an animated horror story and pure fiction. Zombie biology and human-zombie reproduction are not real science. Two real facts inspired it: a fungus that takes over the bodies of ants, and the way a baby's cells can stay in its mother for decades.
