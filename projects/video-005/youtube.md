### Description draft
It begins with a ten-second video. By morning, the government has issued an emergency warning.

What if a zombie outbreak followed real biology, real mathematics and real population data? We follow two students, Hikari and Ren, from Hour Zero to Day Forty: a hospital lockdown, gridlocked bridges, empty supermarkets, a failing power grid, and a radio message from a shelter in the hills.

Along the way: how rabies travels to the brain, the fungus that turns ants into "zombies", the 2009 mathematical study "When Zombies Attack!", Cornell's 2015 simulation of a zombie outbreak across the United States, and the CDC's real zombie-themed preparedness guide.

The story is fiction. Zombies are not real. The science that inspired it is.

If the world changed tonight, what would you do? Tell us in the comments.
