D-D-R six is coming... but your next gaming PC might still be stuck with D-D-R five.

A major new RAM generation has just been previewed, and its performance targets are seriously impressive. It's called D-D-R six. The new standard is targeting more than fifty percent higher memory bandwidth than D-D-R five at launch.

But here's the catch. D-D-R six is still under development. This doesn't mean you can buy it for your gaming PC tomorrow. And even when it arrives, you'll need compatible processors and motherboards to use it. So your existing D-D-R five motherboard won't magically support it.

And there's another problem. While everyone is waiting for faster memory, AI data centres are consuming enormous amounts of memory and putting pressure on the entire industry. That means D-D-R six could bring impressive performance, but it won't automatically make today's RAM cheaper.

So here's the real question. Would you rather have faster D-D-R six... or affordable D-D-R five that you can actually buy? Because right now, gamers might need the second one more.
