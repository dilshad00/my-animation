| **Motion rule** | Every scene has a camera move, at least one element entering on a narration cue, and ambient motion. No scene is a static image. |

## 6. Audio plan

**Mix rule (your instruction): narration is always louder than effects and music.**
Measured narration: speech averages **-26.4 dBFS**, peaks at **-2.4 dBFS**.
- The voice stays at full level. There is no headroom to raise it, so everything else goes under it.
- Effects under speech: at least **12 dB below** the voice.
- Ambience under speech: at least **18 dB below** the voice.
- Big hits go in the gaps listed below and stay well under the voice's peak.
- Each cue's volume is set from that file's measured loudness, and the preview's mix is measured again before you see it.
