### Description draft
What if every storm on Earth went silent, and then Zeus walked down from Mount Olympus? We use the real Greek myths as the rulebook for a fictional modern-day scenario: how governments, scientists, believers and ordinary people might react, what happens when the other Olympians return, and why the king of the gods would come back at all.

The Greek myths in this video are traditional stories, not historical events. The modern-day events are entirely fictional.
