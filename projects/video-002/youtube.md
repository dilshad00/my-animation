## Description
What if every storm on Earth went silent, and then Zeus walked down from Mount Olympus?

He has lightning, an army of gods, and thousands of years of grudges. We have satellites, lawyers and nuclear weapons. Using the real Greek myths as the rulebook, this animated what-if follows the king of the gods into the modern world: how governments, scientists and ordinary people react, what happens when Poseidon and the other Olympians return, and why he really came back. Stay for the ending.

Would you worship him, fear him, challenge him, or open the door? Tell us in the comments.

The Greek myths in this video are traditional stories, not historical events. The modern-day events are entirely fictional.
