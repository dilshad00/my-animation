# Where Does Electricity Come From?

<!-- Voiceover script for the demo. Each ## section is one scene in storyboard.json. -->

## s01-intro
Have you ever wondered where electricity actually comes from?

## s02-switch
Every time you flip a switch, a tiny miracle of engineering happens in an instant.

## s03-sun
It begins one hundred and fifty million kilometers away, with our Sun.

## s04-harvest
Solar panels catch that sunlight, while wind turbines turn moving air into power.

## s05-grid
That energy then races through a vast grid of cables, all the way to your home.

## s06-mix
Today, nearly a third of the world's electricity already comes from renewable sources.

## s07-world
And new solar and wind farms are switching on across the planet every single day.

## s08-outro
So the next time you flip that switch, remember the incredible journey behind it!
